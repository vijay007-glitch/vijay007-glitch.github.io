      The High-NA trade-off

      Resolution scales with λ/NA. Depth of focus scales with λ/NA². Raising NA buys the first and pays with the second.

      Resolution scale, λ/NA smaller prints finer
      Focus scale, λ/NA² larger is more forgiving

      How the model works

      Imaging

      Abbe's method. The illumination is split into 14 to 49 source points. Each one lights the mask from a slightly different angle, the projection lens passes only the spatial frequencies inside its pupil (radius NA/λ), and the intensities from all source points add up. That is why the illumination shape matters: off-axis light lets a dense grating's first diffraction orders through.

      Focus

      Defocus adds the phase 2π·(z/λ)·(√(n² − NA²ρ²) − n) across the pupil, with n = 1.44 for water immersion and 1 for EUV in vacuum. The Bossung plot sweeps focus and measures the CD at the CD site for the nominal dose and ±5%.

      Resist and scoring

      Resist prints wherever dose × intensity exceeds 30% of the clear-field intensity. Edge placement error is measured at gauges spaced along every target edge, the way an OPC verification run does it. Stars use the worst gauge: ★ ≤ 25% of CD, ★★ ≤ 15%, ★★★ ≤ 10%, with no bridges, breaks or stray prints.

      What it leaves out

      Scalar, thin-mask optics on a 128 × 128 periodic grid: no polarization, no 3D mask shadowing (which matters for EUV), no flare, no resist blur or stochastics. Accurate enough to build intuition, not to tape out.
